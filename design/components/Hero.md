# Hero

The live site's opening: a full-width site photograph that fades into night, then an eyebrow, a dandelion display headline, the place and date, and stacked buttons.

**Consumer provides:** one landscape photograph of a real site (Western Cape, real people or plantings, never stock), its alt text, the eyebrow, headline (two to four words), supporting lines and one or two actions.

- The fade is the live site's gradient: transparent at 42% down to `night` at 100%. A second, warmer fade (`night` to `wheat`) exists on the live site for photo tops; use it sparingly.
- Headline in `display` (`display-xl` at 1024px and wider), capitals, `dandelion`. Eyebrow and supporting lines in `paper`.
- The preview uses a seed as a stand-in where the photograph goes.
- Don't put text over the photograph itself; text always sits on night below it.
