# NextPlanting

The live site's "Next planting" section on paper: eyebrow and display headline in night, two or three short `lead` lines with the date and place in `lead-strong`, then stacked actions.

**Consumer provides:** the next Plantings entry (date, site, what to bring) and the sign-up link. With no planting scheduled, swap the copy for "Next planting coming soon" and a newsletter sign-up; never leave a past date up.

- Voice: warm, first person plural, short sentences. The live copy is the model: "Bring gloves if you've got them. We'll bring everything else."
- Keep it to three `lead` lines at most; `lead` has tight leading and is not for paragraphs.
