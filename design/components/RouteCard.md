# RouteCard

Intentional addition for the Get involved hub: a whole-card link on night with the route name in dandelion capitals, one line saying who it's for, and an arrow.

**Consumer provides:** the route name (matching the button text used elsewhere), one line of who it's for, and the link.

- Five routes at most, in the order Join a planting, Dedicate a tree, Donate, Propose a site, Partner with us.
- Hover lights the border in `dandelion`. The whole card is the link; don't nest buttons inside it.
