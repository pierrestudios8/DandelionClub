# SeedBand

The live site's yellow interlude: a `dandelion` band tiled with small paper seeds (`seed-pattern-paper.svg` at 144px), with one large night seed in the middle. It separates sections and carries the brand when there are no words.

**Consumer provides:** nothing except placement. It is decorative, so mark it `aria-hidden`.

- One per page at most. Never put text on it.
- The large seed is `seed-night.svg`. It is the Rive slot for the taproot story (a later phase), so keep the band's height (about 390px) when animating.
