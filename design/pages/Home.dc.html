What we do

More than trees.

Our work

Food forests

Layered plantings of fruit trees, shrubs and ground cover on school land.

Water-smart soil

Mulch, swales and planting that hold winter rain through a dry summer.

Local enterprise

Support for women and young people starting food businesses around each site.

Outdoor learning

The food forest as a classroom for teachers, learners and neighbours.

Compost and circularity

Food scraps and garden waste turned back into soil on site.

Heritage and belonging

Local plant knowledge and the people who hold it, at the centre of each site.
