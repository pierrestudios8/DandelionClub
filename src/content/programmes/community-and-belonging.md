---
title: Community & belonging
summary: Food forests are places people grow together. They create opportunities to share knowledge, stories, skills, food and responsibility for a common place through cultural immersion and team building events, training and leadership development circles.
order: 6
---

Not all knowledge sits in textbooks. Communities carry knowledge of plants, food, medicine, land, culture and place. We want our projects to create space for that knowledge too.

A food forest can become somewhere stories are remembered, traditional plants are grown, knowledge moves between generations and people build a deeper relationship with the place around them.
