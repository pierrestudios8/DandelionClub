---
title: Green enterprises & cooperatives
summary: A productive landscape can create more than food. Seedling and tree propagation nurseries, fruit and vegetable production, green products, compost and green entrepreneurship development can create pathways towards local livelihoods.
order: 5
---

A food forest can also become an economic ecosystem. Produce can be sold. Seedlings can be propagated. Cuttings can become plants. Organic waste can become compost. Skills can become services. Knowledge can become training.

Our longer-term ambition is to help communities identify enterprises that naturally emerge from the ecological systems they are already caring for.
