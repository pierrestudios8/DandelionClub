---
title: Healthy soil & circular systems
summary: We compost, mulch, process Bokashi and find ways to return organic resources to the soil instead of treating them as waste.
order: 2
---

Healthy landscapes begin with healthy soil. We use composting, Bokashi, mulch, vermiculture and other biological processes to turn organic material into a resource.

Where possible, we connect waste streams to places that can use them. Food waste becomes compost. Prunings become mulch. Plant residues return to the soil.

The aim is simple: keep nutrients cycling.
