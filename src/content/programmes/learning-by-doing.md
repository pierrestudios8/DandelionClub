---
title: Learning by doing
summary: Our gardens become outdoor classrooms where children, growers, volunteers and community members can learn about food, ecology, soil, biodiversity and how natural systems work.
order: 4
---

Food forests make ecology tangible. Instead of learning about ecosystems only from a textbook, children can stand inside one.

They can see pollination happen. Watch compost become soil. Understand water cycles. Plant something. Harvest something. Taste something.

And see how their actions affect a living system.
