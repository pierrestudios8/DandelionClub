---
title: Water-wise growing
summary: We design for increasingly water-stressed communities, capturing water, improving soil and exploring practical regenerative growing systems.
order: 3
---

Water scarcity increasingly shapes what can be grown and where. We explore solutions that reduce demand, improve soil-water retention and make better use of available water.

Different sites may involve rainwater harvesting, wicking systems, aquaponics, soil regeneration, drought-tolerant planting or experimental approaches to difficult water conditions.
